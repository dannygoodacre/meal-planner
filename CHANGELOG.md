## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-24

- **Your food library.** Add the foods you eat with their nutrition per serving: calories, protein, carbs (and sugars), fat (and saturates), fibre, and salt. Measure by weight in grams or by count in pieces, and keep a link to where the numbers came from.
- **Meals.** Build a meal from foods in your library and set how much of each goes in. The nutrition updates as you go.
- **Meal plans.** Plan a full day by stacking meals together. Pull in a meal you've already saved or make one up on the spot, then adjust its ingredients just for that plan. Expand a meal to see what's in it.
- **Nutrition at a glance.** Every meal and plan shows running totals, plus how much of the energy comes from protein, carbs, and fat.
- **Search everywhere.** Find foods, meals, and plans as you type, with a quick macro summary next to each result.
- **Add a food without leaving the page.** If an ingredient isn't in your library yet, add it from the search box and it drops straight into your meal.
- **No more duplicates.** As you name a new food, meal, or plan, you'll see whether that name is free, already taken, or close to something you already have.
- **Edit foods and plans.** Open a food or a saved plan, make your changes and save, or cancel to put everything back. Fix a food's nutrition and every meal and plan that uses it updates to match.
- **Helpful checks.** Forms point out missing names, empty meals, and impossible numbers before you save.
